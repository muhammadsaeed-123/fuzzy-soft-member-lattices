# Interpretation and scope

The theoretical algorithms are exact for their finite inputs. This does not validate the semantic choice of coordination maps, utility weights, source/target rules or observational variables. DataCo is one historical public benchmark, not a causal experiment or a physical multi-echelon network. Realized and derived attributes are not automatically controllable. The dependency audit verifies accounting identities; it does not report a DataCo coordinate-removal intervention. Synthetic comparisons describe one seeded realization. Fixed-size subsets may differ in membership, and the deterministic order-identifier tie rule is a declared convention, not a uniquely justified preference.

The synchronized numerical illustration is expressly defined by the two printed chains and an arithmetic-mean utility. It is not an attempted reconstruction of an unavailable scoring specification.
